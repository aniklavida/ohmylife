We lost power for most of the afternoon, so we sat on the veranda and
listened to the rain instead of the television. Nani made tea on the
kerosene stove and told the story about her brother and the stolen
rickshaw again, the same way she always tells it, like it happened
last week.

I keep this one because of the sound the rain made on the tin roof,
not because anything happened.
