We only meant to grab a quick coffee before Rumi's train, and then
neither of us checked the time until the place started stacking chairs
around us. Talked about her new manager, my mother's knee, nothing
that mattered and everything that did.

She missed the train. Took the next one. Didn't seem to mind.
